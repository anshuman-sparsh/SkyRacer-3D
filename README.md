# 🏎️ SkyRacer 3D

### Fly Fast. Collect Rings. Survive.

**SkyRacer 3D** is a free-to-play, futuristic 3D arcade racing game that runs directly in your browser.

Pilot a high-speed hovercraft through an endless neon-lit world, collect energy rings, avoid dangerous obstacles, and compete for a place on the global leaderboard.

No downloads. No installations. Just pure arcade action.

**🎮 [PLAY SKYRACER 3D](https://www.skyracer.online/)**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🚀 High-Speed Arcade Gameplay

Experience fast-paced flying with smooth controls, responsive movement, dynamic camera effects, and increasing speed as you progress.

### 🌌 Futuristic 3D Environment

Race through an endless synthwave-inspired world featuring glowing tracks, vibrant neon visuals, atmospheric effects, and procedurally generated scenery.

### 💎 Energy Rings & Speed Boosts

Collect glowing rings to earn bonus points and increase your speed.

- **Blue Rings:** Award points and provide a temporary automatic boost.
- **Pink Rings:** Award points and trigger a powerful speed surge.
- **Speed Progression:** Collect more rings to gradually increase your normal cruising speed.

### 🎯 Flight Guidance System

An intelligent flight marker helps you navigate upcoming rings.

- Appears when a nearby ring is within your current flight lane.
- Stays ahead of your aircraft without jumping between rings.
- Uses green and amber indicators to show flight-path alignment.
- Provides visual depth references to help judge ring positions.

### ⏱️ Ring Survival Challenge

Keep collecting rings to stay in the race.

You have **30 seconds** to collect a ring. After 20 seconds without a pickup, a visible 10-second countdown begins. If the timer reaches zero, your flight ends.

Every successful ring collection resets the countdown.

### 🏆 Global Leaderboard

Compete with players worldwide and see how your best run compares.

- Global Top 50 rankings.
- Custom player nicknames.
- Personal best score tracking.
- Online score submission.
- Anonymous player authentication — no traditional account signup required.

### 📱 Desktop & Mobile Support

Play using keyboard controls on desktop or intuitive touch gestures on your phone or tablet.

### 🔊 Dynamic Audio

Enjoy engine sounds, collection effects, and high-speed audio feedback generated directly in the browser.

## 💯 Scoring System

Earn points by travelling farther and collecting rings.

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Distance Travelled | 10 points per world unit |
| Ring Collected | 50 bonus points |

**Final Score = ⌊(Distance × 10) + (Rings × 50)⌋**

Your highest score and best ring-collection record are also tracked locally.

Challenge yourself to survive longer, collect more rings, and climb the global rankings!
