HOW TO PLAY

Fly through glowing rings, earn points, and dodge orange barriers. Hit a barrier or miss the ring deadline and your run ends.

On a phone or tablet

Move: Touch and drag on the game. Drag left or right to steer, and up or down to change height.

Go faster: Press and hold BOOST at the bottom-right. Let go to stop the manual boost.

On a computer

Move: Use W A S D or the arrow keys. W / Up climbs; S / Down descends.

Go faster: Hold Space or Shift. Release to stop the manual boost.

Pause: Press P or Esc.

What the rings do

Blue ring: Gives bonus points and a short automatic speed boost.

Pink ring: Gives bonus points and a stronger speed surge.

Collecting more rings also increases your normal speed over time.

Ring deadline: Collect any ring within 30 seconds to keep flying. After 20 seconds without a ring, a visible 10-second countdown begins. Every collected ring resets the timer. Pausing also pauses the countdown.

Aim, score, and energy

Flight marker: Appears only when an upcoming ring is close and in your aircraft's current flight lane. It stays ahead of your aircraft without jumping to the ring. Green means your projected path is aligned; amber means adjust your flight. The eligible ring's vertical line shows its height above the track.

Score: Get 10 points per world unit travelled and 50 per ring. Your best local records stay on this device.

Energy Boost bar: Shows the time left on a blue ring's automatic boost. Holding BOOST is separate.

Pause, restart, and sound

Use Pause / Resume to take a break, and Restart after a crash.

Use Audio On / Off to turn game sounds on or off.

Opening this guide during a run pauses the game. Close it, then choose Resume to keep flying.
